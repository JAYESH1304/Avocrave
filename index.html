01 — About

Good food should
make you feel good.

We're AvoCrave — where avocado meets the irresistible world of street food.

AvoCrave was started by a group of S.Y. BMS students as a college project, with a simple idea: to break the boring perception of avocado and turn it into something exciting, flavourful, and fun.

We wanted to take avocado beyond its usual image and give it a creative street-food twist. Our menu brings together familiar favourites with the creamy goodness of avocado, creating unique combinations that are both delicious and exciting. From our Avocado Momos, Avocado Sev Puri, and Avocado KitKat Smoothie, every product is designed to give you a completely different way to experience avocado.
